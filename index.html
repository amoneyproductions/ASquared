Project

Sweet Wheels

Client
Sweet Wheels Ice Cream
Where
Fairfield County, CT
Scope
Logo, colors, and the truck design

What came of it

  1. 1 Name turned into a logo, a set of colors, and a truck design
  2. 5 Colors chosen once and used on every part of the truck
  3. 63 Likes and comments on the launch post, mostly from locals
  4. $35K+ First-season sales for a business that started as a name and an idea

Sales figure shared by the owner, covering the first season, January to August 2026. We handled the brand and the wrap, not the day to day running of the business.

The owner came to us with a name and a picture in her head of what this truck should feel like. We worked closely with her on all of it: the logo, the colors, and the truck itself, until it looked like the business she had been picturing. It is on the road now.
